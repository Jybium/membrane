#### The 4 Specific Steps You Saw in the Modal:
1. **Witness Private State**: 
   The hospital query ran **locally inside the hospital perimeter**. It counted matching patients (`18 records ≥ 15 required`) without sending raw medical records, names, or individual ages over the internet.
2. **ZK Proof Server (`:6300`)**: 
   The witness count (`18`) was fed into Midnight's private witness circuit (`witness getHospitalPatientsAggregate(): Uint<16>`). The proof server compiled an R1CS zero-knowledge constraint proving `18 ≥ 15` is mathematically true.
3. **Lace Wallet Balancing**: 
   The hospital's Midnight Lace Wallet balanced unshielded dust fees and signed the transaction header without revealing private witness data.
4. **Midnight Node Ledger**: 
   The shielded transaction containing the 256-byte ZK proof was submitted to the Midnight Preview testnet. Midnight validators confirmed that the hospital satisfied the recruitment criteria and registered the enrollment on the ledger.

### 2. How Hospitals Change or Register Their Available Samples

In Membrane's privacy model, hospital data stays **strictly institutional**:

#### A. Institutional EHR Vault (`/vault`)
- Click **Consented EHR Vault** in the top navigation bar.
- This represents the hospital's internal electronic health records system.
- Hospitals filter and inspect their consented patient inventory by diagnosis (e.g., ICD-10 `K30`, `C34`, `J45`, `E11`), age brackets, and consent status.

#### B. Production Integration (FHIR / Hospital Database)
In an enterprise hospital deployment:
1. **Local Hospital Database / EHR Connectors**: The hospital connects their existing database (PostgreSQL, MySQL) or hospital health information system (Epic / Cerner via HL7 FHIR APIs).
2. **Patient Consent Registry**: When patients sign research consent forms, their anonymized records are tagged with a research consent flag.
3. **Automated Predicate Queries**: When research labs publish study requirements, the hospital's local Docker daemon automatically queries internal tables (`SELECT COUNT(*) FROM patients WHERE icd = ? AND age BETWEEN ? AND ? AND consented = true`).
4. **No Centralized Data Repository**: A research lab never gets direct access to the database; they only receive the cryptographic proof.
